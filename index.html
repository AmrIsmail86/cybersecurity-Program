Vision

To become a leading cybersecurity department recognized for excellence in education, research, and community engagement in the field of information security.

Mission

To provide high-quality education and training in cybersecurity, preparing graduates to meet the challenges of securing digital assets and supporting national and global information security goals.

Program Goals

  • Develop skilled professionals in cybersecurity.
  • Promote research and innovation in cybersecurity technologies.
  • Establish partnerships with industry and government sectors.
  • Foster ethical and responsible cybersecurity practices.
