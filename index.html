Cybersecurity Department

Vision Mission Objectives Program Documents Faculty Members

Vision

To be a pioneering academic department in cybersecurity education, research, and innovation at the national and international levels.

Mission

To prepare competent graduates with strong foundations in cybersecurity who are capable of protecting digital assets, ensuring privacy, and leading the future of secure technologies.

Objectives

  • Deliver deep knowledge of cybersecurity principles and technologies.
  • Promote critical thinking and secure software design.
  • Enhance ethical responsibility and professional standards.
  • Encourage research and continuous development in security domains.
